# Leap Content Strategy — The Complete Framework for Authority, Reach & Revenue

By The Leap Lab. Built on 6 proven frameworks: The 3-Step Brand System, The Five 1s Methodology, The SLAY Framework, The Viral Content Blueprint, The Weekly Content Engine, and The Lead Generation Framework.

## Section 1 — Strategic Foundation

### 1.1 The Five 1s — Before You Post a Single Word

Everything begins here. Before creating any content, lock in these five commitments. Violating even one dilutes results.

| The 1 | Your Commitment |
|---|---|
| 1 Target Market | Define one specific, well-defined audience. Speak to everyone = convert no one. |
| 1 Problem | Solve one real, painful problem for that audience. Specificity drives resonance. |
| 1 Offer | One product or service that solves that problem. Complexity kills momentum. |
| 1 Platform | Master one channel completely (LinkedIn) before expanding. Depth beats breadth. |
| 1 Year of Focus | Commit for 12 months minimum. Consistency compounds; dabbling doesn't. |

### 1.2 Your Unique Brand Asset — The Story Vault

AI can replicate information. It cannot replicate lived experience. Personal stories are your most durable competitive advantage.

**The Pause–Reflect–Document Method:**
1. Scroll through the last 60 months of photos and voice notes.
2. Identify at least 10 stories with a clear lesson embedded inside.
3. For each story, ask: What problem does this solve for my audience? What transformation does it demonstrate?
4. Use AI to help refine and distill the core lesson — but never let AI invent the story.

**Key principle**: Relatable beats impressive. Your audience doesn't need to be in awe of you — they need to see the next step in their own evolution reflected in your story.

### 1.3 Traffic Is the Foundation

Traffic — consistent visibility — solves nearly every business problem. The Leap Content Strategy treats daily visibility as non-negotiable infrastructure, not a nice-to-have.

**The Growth Equation**: Daily Visibility + High-Value Content + Strategic CTAs = Scalable Revenue

Post cadence target: 4–5x per week. Absolute minimum: 3x per week. (Reference example: Lara Acosta — 0 → 100K followers in under 18 months posting daily.)

## Section 3 — Post Construction

### 3.1 The SLAY Framework — Every Post, Every Time

The structural backbone of every post. Apply it to all four content pillars.

- **S — Story**: Lines 1–3. Open with a story. Hook the reader emotionally. No lists, no facts. Just scene-setting narrative.
- **L — Lesson**: Line 4. Lead with the lesson. The pivot point — from emotion to insight.
- **A — Action**: Body. Expand with actionable advice. Short listicles. Max 2 sentences per paragraph. White space is your friend.
- **Y — You**: Close. Ask a question that puts the ball in the reader's court. Drives comments. One question only.

**SLAY pro tip**: Think lesson first, story second. The story is the vehicle — not the destination. Emotion makes them remember you. The lesson makes them trust you.

### 3.2 The Hook — The Most Important Lines You'll Write

LinkedIn shows 2–3 lines before the "See More" break. If those lines don't stop the scroll, the post is invisible. The algorithm rewards dwell time — the hook must earn it.

**Hook formulas that consistently win:**

| Hook Type | Formula & Example |
|---|---|
| Confession | "I wasted 3 years managing projects before I realised I was doing leadership completely wrong." |
| Counter-Intuitive | "The most effective PMs I know almost never use Gantt charts." |
| Specific Number | "5 phrases that make stakeholders trust you immediately — and 3 that destroy it." |
| Status Threat | "If you can't do this one thing, you'll be replaced by an AI-assisted junior PM in 2 years." |
| Story Opening | "I was standing at the door of a plane at 12,000 feet. That's when I understood what leadership actually requires." |
| Frustration Hook | "Feeling stuck managing endless projects, even though you plan everything perfectly?" |
| Results Hook | "Ready to lead projects with confidence — without the overwhelm?" |

### 3.3 Post Anatomy for Maximum Dwell Time

Every section serves a specific algorithmic and psychological purpose:

1. **Lines 1–2 — Hook**: Stop the scroll. One provocative sentence. No bullet points here.
2. **Line 3 — Bridge**: Make a promise. Tell them what they'll gain by reading on.
3. **[White space]** — Breathe. Short paragraphs only, max 2 sentences each. Line breaks are free engagement.
4. **Body**: Deliver the value — story, lesson, and action points using the SLAY structure.
5. **Insight**: The "earned" payoff. Your unique perspective. This is what gets shared.
6. **[White space]** — Breathe again. Signal the close is coming.
7. **CTA**: ONE action only — comment, save, or click a link. Never ask for all three.

## Section 4 — Viral Content Formats

Use 2 of these formats per week to inject algorithmic fuel into the content mix. The viral formula: **Emotion + Relatability + Shareability**.

1. **Unpopular Opinion** (⚡ 5–10x reach) — Hook: "Unpopular opinion: [bold true claim]." Context: why it matters → personal story example → key insight → "Agree or disagree? Comment below."
2. **Framework Carousel** (⚡ 3–7x reach, 20–30% save rate) — 7–10 slides: bold promise on slide 1 → each step with visual on slides 2–8 → summary slide → CTA: "Save this + follow for more."
3. **Truth Bomb List** (⚡ 4–8x reach, high share rate) — "10 things nobody tells you about being a PM:" with relatable truths (humour + vulnerability). Screenshot-worthy, fills a knowledge gap.
4. **Before / After** (⚡ 3–6x reach, aspirational) — Side-by-side: "How I used to run projects vs. how I run them now." Visual table or split-screen design.
5. **Ask Me Anything** (⚡ 5–10x reach, community builder) — Open question: "I've led 50+ projects across 3 industries. Ask me anything." Respond to every comment thoughtfully — positions you as the go-to expert.

## Section 5 — Lead Generation: Hook → Value → Leap

Every piece of content is either building trust or converting it. The Lead Generation Framework turns engaged readers into warm leads using a three-part structure.

### 5.1 The Three-Part Formula

| Part | Goal | Formula | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hook (Frustration) | Stop scroll + self-identify | "Feeling [pain] even though you [effort]?" | "Feeling burned out managing endless projects, even with all the right tools?" |
| Hook (Results) | Build aspiration | "Ready to [result] without [pain]?" | "Ready to lead with confidence — without the overwhelm?" |
| Value Proposition | Deliver substance + credibility | "Join [offer] to learn how to improve [3 areas]." | "Join The Leap Lab to master courage, clarity, and growth through every project." |
| CTA (The Leap) | Inspire action | "Take the leap → [transformation]" | "Take the leap → Join 1,000+ Project Managers growing with courage." |

### 5.2 CTA Progression by Growth Stage

| Stage | CTA Type & Purpose |
|---|---|
| Starting Out (0–500 followers) | DM me — have real conversations, understand how your audience thinks and speaks. |
| Building Traction (500–5K) | Opt-in to a free resource — waiting list, brand assessment, free toolkit, or mini-course. |
| Scaling (5K+) | Automated funnel — webinar, mini-course, or newsletter that collects name, email, and phone. |
| Revenue Mode | Sales call or special offer — content pre-sells, the CTA converts. |

## Section 6 — Brand Building & Scaling System

### 6.1 The 3-Phase Growth Trajectory

| Phase | Focus & Actions |
|---|---|
| Phase 1: Discover (Months 1–3) | Run Pause–Reflect–Document. Identify 10 signature stories. Post 3x/week using Pain, News, and Prize hooks. DM everyone who engages. |
| Phase 2: Traction (Months 4–8) | Post 4–5x/week. Introduce viral formats 2x/week. Launch an opt-in (toolkit, assessment, or mini-course). Build your email list. |
| Phase 3: Scale (Month 9+) | Post daily. Automate the lead funnel. Drive audience from short-form content → signature long-form asset (book, YouTube, newsletter). Replace DMs with automated sequences. |

### 6.2 The Content Funnel Architecture

| Level | Format | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Top of Funnel | Short-form posts: story posts, opinion posts, truth bombs, AMAs | Reach, awareness, algorithm boost — attract cold audiences. |
| Middle of Funnel | Framework carousels, how-to lists, before/after posts | Build trust and authority — warm up your audience. |
| Bottom of Funnel | Soft CTA posts, lead-gen posts, testimonial stories | Convert warm followers into email subscribers and paying clients. |

### 6.3 The Long-Form Signature Asset

Short-form content creates reach. Long-form assets create authority. Drive the audience from LinkedIn posts to one core signature asset:

- **Book or E-Book**: highest-authority format — referenced, shared, remembered.
- **YouTube Channel**: long-form video demonstrating process and personality.
- **Newsletter**: owned audience, not subject to algorithm changes — highest conversion channel.
- **Podcast**: deep trust builder for a listening audience.

**The funnel flow**: Daily Short-Form Post (LinkedIn) → Link to Long-Form Asset → Opt-in / Email Capture → Email Sequence → Sales Call or Special Offer → Paying Client.

### 7.3 Hook Quality Test — Ask Before Every Post

- [ ] Does Line 1 create an immediate emotion (frustration, curiosity, or aspiration)?
- [ ] Would someone stop scrolling for this in 0.5 seconds?

- [ ] Is it free of bullet points, headers, or listicle signals in the first 3 lines?
