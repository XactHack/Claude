# My Story — A Life of Adventure, Growth & Reinvention

## From South Africa to the Skies

Life doesn't come with a map. It's a wild, unpredictable journey filled with peaks, valleys, and moments that force you to leap into the unknown. My story is one of reinventions — of trading comfort for courage, fear for freedom, and stagnation for soaring. It's about finding the strength to let go of who you're supposed to be and embracing who you're meant to become.

This is the story of how a kid from South Africa traded a desk job for the skies, and a life of "what ifs" for a life of "why not."

## The Beginning: Roots and Resilience

Born June 30, 1982, in Johannesburg, South Africa, to two extraordinary parents who taught the value of love, hard work, and resilience. Family — two younger brothers, the sister I never had, and two amazing nephews — has always been my anchor, my safe harbour even when my choices seemed reckless or unconventional.

Growing up, I was drawn to two things: technology and discipline. At seven, I started karate, earning my Junior Black Belt by 14. Squash became my next obsession, competing at provincial level in high school. Then Wing Chun Kung Fu entered my life, bringing a new sense of purpose — years of dedication led to representing South Africa at the Kung Fu World Championships in Singapore, winning silver and bronze medals. A defining moment: discipline and passion could take me anywhere.

But life had other plans.

## The Curveballs: Injury, Heartbreak, and Stagnation

A severe knee injury during a Kung Fu demonstration left me physically and emotionally drained. Around the same time, my parents divorced and a painful breakup shook my world. My IT career, once a source of pride, felt stagnant. I was stuck in an unhealthy cycle, running from problems instead of facing them.

I was lost. But sometimes it takes a leap of faith — literally — to find your way back.

## The Turning Point: Freefall and Freedom

On a whim, I tried tandem skydiving. Standing at the edge of the plane, 12,000 feet above the earth, heart racing. The instructor counted down: 3… 2… 1… and we jumped.

The rush of wind, the blur of the ground below, the sheer exhilaration of freefall — electrifying. In that moment, everything else faded away. The fear, the doubt, the pain — gone. For the first time in years, I felt alive.

When my feet touched the ground, I knew I had found what I was searching for.

## The Bold Move: Chasing the Dream

I enrolled in an AFF skydiving course, earned my certification, and began skydiving on my own. It became an obsession — weekends once spent on parties and alcohol were now dedicated to saving for the next jump.

Indoor skydiving opened a new world of possibilities. I traveled to Prague to train at one of the best facilities in the world and realized: you could get paid to teach people how to fly. That ignited a fire.

So I made the boldest decision of my life: turned my back on a 12-year IT career, sold everything I owned, and moved to the UK to pursue Body Flight as a profession. My family thought I was losing my mind. Parts of me wondered if I was too. But I craved adventure, and I was determined to chase it.

## The Pinnacle: Representing Bahrain and Beyond

For two years, I honed my skills as an indoor skydiving instructor in the UK, traveling and skydiving across Europe. Then a new opportunity arose in Bahrain, where a state-of-the-art indoor skydiving facility was opening. I joined as a senior instructor, climbing the ranks to operations manager and earning sponsorship to train and compete internationally. Representing Bahrain at the World Championships was a pinnacle moment — a dream realized.

## The Full Circle: Returning to Tech with a New Perspective

An opportunity in Abu Dhabi called — a chance to be part of CLYMB, the largest indoor skydiving facility in the world. For six years Abu Dhabi has been home, rising to CLYMB operations manager and pushing the boundaries of what was possible in the sport. Over time, the familiar tug of restlessness returned. My father's passing and a nasty skydiving incident forced a reevaluation of path.

Then an unexpected opportunity emerged: a senior IT project manager role within the wider organization behind CLYMB (Miral). A chance to come full circle, back to the tech world left behind. Despite the challenges of reentering an industry that had evolved exponentially, I took the leap.

3 years in, it's been one of the most demanding yet rewarding experiences of my life — and the wins have been real. While earning my PMP, ITIL Foundation, and two Generative AI certifications (Google Cloud and PMI), I was simultaneously putting those skills to work in the field:

- Led the full digital ecosystem onboarding for **TeamLab Phenomena** — one of the most complex multi-system integrations Miral had ever attempted, weaving together ticketing, CRM, ERP, biometrics, retail, infrastructure, and more into a seamless launch of a globally recognized attraction. On time. Under budget. A blueprint for what comes next.
- Directed the digital expansion of **Yas Waterworld**.
- Modernized connectivity infrastructure across four flagship parks — Ferrari World, SeaWorld, Warner Bros. World, and Yas Waterworld.
- Led the transformation of **Miral's entire IT service management function**, deploying a 14-module ServiceNow platform and upskilling 90 team members — taking IT from reactive support desk to strategic business enabler.

It turns out the same mindset that gets you to jump out of a plane at 12,000 feet — trust the preparation, execute under pressure, stick the landing — works just as well in enterprise tech delivery.

## Today

Energized and focused. Back on the horse and skydiving again, studying for PgMP certification, learning Arabic, and playing the guitar. Feeling fit and healthy, developed a habit for running, taken up squash again. Keen to learn to surf, embracing adventure wherever it can be found. Working to overcome insecurities, connect deeply with others, absorb knowledge, lean into growth — making a positive impact is high on the agenda.
